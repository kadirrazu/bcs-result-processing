# Choice Optimization CO3.10 — Minimal OMR Review + Full Individual Trace

- The OMR batch review page is decision-focused and follows the Choice Validation module's compact review pattern.
- Long `Expansion / Removal Details` and eligibility evidence are removed from the batch page.
- Review-required candidates retain the three meaningful comparison lineages: Registration Choice, Finalized Validated Choice, and OMR Options.
- A new individual OMR Choice Details page preserves full lineage and explains every kept, removed, and expanded choice.
- The individual page exposes human-readable reason codes/messages, optional eligibility evidence, operator registration/decision resolutions, and approved effective-choice reason/source.
- Raw OMR source remains immutable; derived validation and effective choice remain traceable.
